Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Paybill number?
No. A Till Number works perfectly. We support both Paybill (CustomerPayBillOnline) and Buy Goods (CustomerBuyGoodsOnline). If you don't have either, we'll help you set one up.
What is the transaction fee?
A small percentage of each M-Pesa payment goes to the platform. It decreases as you upgrade: 1% on Starter, down to 0.25% on Enterprise. There are no hidden fees — you keep everything else.
What happens when I exceed my SMS quota?
Additional SMS are billed per message at the overage rate for your plan (Ksh 2–3/SMS). You'll never be cut off mid-month — overages are billed at the next invoice.
Can I change my plan later?
Yes. You can upgrade at any time and we'll prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
What is the setup fee?
A one-time fee paid when we first set up your account. It covers initial configuration, M-Pesa integration, bundle setup, and onboarding. There's no setup fee for the Trial.
Is there a contract?
No contracts. Pay monthly and cancel anytime. Annual plans are paid upfront and are non-refundable but save you 2 months of fees.
What types of businesses does this work for?
WiFi hotspots, hotels & guesthouses, parking lots, shops, rental properties, salons, gyms, and any business that accepts M-Pesa. If your customers pay with mobile money, AutomateKE works for you.
