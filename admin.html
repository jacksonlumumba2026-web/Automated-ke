WELCOME TO AUTOMATEKE

Your platform is ready. Add your first client to start seeing real revenue data. Each client pays you a monthly fee and you automate their entire business.

1

Add your first client

Click "Add Client" above. Fill in their business details. They'll get a dashboard link immediately.

2

Configure M-Pesa credentials

Go to API Config and add your Safaricom Daraja keys. Get them free at developer.safaricom.co.ke →

3

Configure SMS (Africa's Talking)

Sign up free at africastalking.com → then add your API key in settings.

Deploy backend server

Upload server files to Truehost VPS (Ksh 500/mo). Your system goes fully live and automated.
